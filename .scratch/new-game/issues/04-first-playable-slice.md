What is the first testable version?

## Answer
Ascendant Nodes is a top-down browser action RPG prototype. The player moves with WASD or arrow keys, aims with the mouse, casts projectiles with click or Space, defeats pursuing void creatures, gathers essence, collects node pickups, and chooses an ascension upgrade after earning enough essence. The vessel can lose vitality and reincarnate through the restart flow.
