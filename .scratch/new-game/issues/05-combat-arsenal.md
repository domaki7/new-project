# Combat arsenal

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How does the overlord build power during combat?

## Answer
The vessel begins with the ranged Star Bolt and can collect or choose additional node weapons. Void Blade is melee, while Gravity Nova, Soul Chain, and Royal Meteor provide area, chaining, and impact attacks. Every equipped weapon auto-casts on the nearest enemy using its own cooldown, so combinations create different combat builds.

The enemy roster now includes Wisps, Brutes, Chargers, Splitters, and Leeches with distinct speed, durability, contact damage, and essence rewards.
