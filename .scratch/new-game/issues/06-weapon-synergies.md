# Weapon synergies

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How should multiple auto-cast weapons combine?

## Answer
Every equipped weapon auto-casts independently at the nearest enemy. Weapon combinations create extra build identity: Void Blade makes Star Bolt pierce, Soul Chain strengthens Gravity Nova, and Crown of the Absolute strengthens Royal Meteor. The player can collect weapons in the arena and choose additional weapons at ascension.

The enemy roster includes Wisp, Brute, Charger, Splitter, and Leech variants with distinct combat stats and rewards.
