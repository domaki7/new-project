# Cartoon knight and monsters

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How should the combatants read visually?

## Answer
The player is a stylized crowned knight with armor, helmet, visor, plume, shield-like body armor, and a sword silhouette. Monsters use family-specific cartoon shapes: wisps are diamond spirits, brutes and the Regent are armored rounded hulks, chargers are horned arrowheads, splitters are divided blobs, and leeches are elongated creatures. Their color, eye marks, shadows, and health bars preserve combat readability.
