# Arsenal and upgrades

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How should the player build a larger arsenal?

## Answer
The arsenal now includes Void Blade, Star Bolt, Gravity Nova, Soul Chain, Royal Meteor, Frost Lance, Venom Orb, and Storm Sigil. Every weapon auto-casts independently at the nearest enemy. Frost slows, Venom applies damage over time, and Storm strikes several nearby enemies.

Ascension choices now mix weapon nodes with permanent upgrades: War Blessing increases damage, Quickened Rite reduces cooldowns, Iron Soul increases maximum vitality, and Gravity Hand increases pickup range. This creates meaningful choices between adding a weapon and improving the current build.
