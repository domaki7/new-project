# Ascension upgrades

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How can the player specialize beyond collecting weapons?

## Answer
Ascension choices now include permanent build upgrades: Executioner Rite gives a chance for double damage, Blood of Stars regenerates vitality, Crownward extends damage invulnerability, War Blessing increases weapon damage, Quickened Rite reduces cooldowns, Iron Soul increases maximum vitality, Gravity Hand increases pickup range, and Arsenal Mastery increases damage for every equipped weapon.
