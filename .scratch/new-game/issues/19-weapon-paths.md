# Weapon paths

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How should melee and ranged builds stay distinct?

## Answer
The vessel starts with no weapon. A nearby labeled starter relic is guaranteed on the floor, and collecting it establishes the run as MELEE or RANGED. Later weapon nodes and passive upgrades are filtered to that family. Duplicate weapons still evolve by rank. Floor relics show their full name and family label before collection so the player can understand the choice.
