# Simultaneous arsenal

Type: prototype
Status: resolved

How many weapons can the player equip, and do they attack together?

## Answer
The player starts unarmed and chooses a melee or ranged path with the first weapon pickup. Up to five distinct weapons from that family can be equipped simultaneously. Every equipped weapon auto-casts independently at the nearest enemy. Duplicate pickups rank up the existing weapon instead of consuming another weapon slot.

Ranged relics show their full name and MELEE or RANGED family label on the floor before collection.
